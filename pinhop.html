Pinhop: Geography Quiz Game

A daily geography flight. Read the clue, spin the globe until the destination sits under the pin, and land. The closer you touch down, the more you score.

Coming soon to theApp Store

Who it's for

  • Travellers, map lovers and anyone who likes a geography quiz
  • Aviation fans who know their airport codes
  • Anyone who wants a quick daily puzzle with a streak to keep
  • Free to play, supported by ads

What's on board

  1. Daily Flight: five destinations a day, the same for everyone, from famous to tricky
  2. Four kinds of clue: a city, an airport code, a landmark or a flag, plus a daily theme
  3. Fuel Run: an endless mode where misses burn fuel and smooth landings top it up
  4. Passport stamps: collect 483 cities, earn pilot ranks and unlock new planes
  5. Leaderboards and a widget: compare scores through Game Center and keep your streak on your Home Screen
