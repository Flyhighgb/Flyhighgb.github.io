    How Quick Play works

    Pick a region and you get ten airport codes, chosen at random. For each one, pick the airport it belongs to from four choices. The wrong answers come from the same part of the world, so you can't just guess the continent.

    At the end you'll see your score and the codes you missed, so you know which ones to remember. Your best score for each region is saved in this browser.

    Want a challenge everyone shares? Try Daily Departure: the same five codes for everyone, once a day.
