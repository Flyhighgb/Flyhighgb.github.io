Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers Pinhop for iPhone, Flight Deck for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the iPhone and iPad apps Airport Codes Quiz and Airline Tails Quiz (the "apps"), published by Amateurgames1. It explains what information is used when you play and why.

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your scores and settings are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

In Flight Deck, the pilot callsign you choose, your progress, coins and unlocked aircraft are also kept only on your device.

In Pinhop, your streak, scores, passport stamps, unlocked planes and settings are kept only on your device. The Pinhop Home Screen widget reads your streak from the app on the same device; nothing is sent anywhere to show it.

Pinhop map lookups

Pinhop never asks for or uses your own location. When you land, the map position of your pin (not where you are) is sent to Apple's map service to find out which country it landed in, so we can give you credit for landing in the right country. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you're offline, this check is skipped.

Game Center and Google Play Games

Pinhop and Flight Deck can use Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and Game Center nickname are shared with Apple to show the leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your Apple ID.

On Android, Flight Deck can use Google Play Games for leaderboards in the same way. If you are signed in to Play Games, your scores and Play Games profile name are shared with Google under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive your Google account details.

In-app purchases

Flight Deck offers an optional "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow it, Flight Deck and Pinhop show daily streak reminders. These reminders are created on your device and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's Settings.

Advertising

The apps are free and show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address, device type, operating system, ad interactions and your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad, only if you allow tracking when asked). Google's use of this information is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

On iPhone and iPad, you can turn off personalised ads at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

On Android, Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete it at any time in your device's settings, usually under Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices Settings > Google > Ads).

In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Flight Deck and Pinhop ask for your consent using Google's consent form before any ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in Flight Deck under Settings > Advert privacy choices, and in Pinhop under Settings > Ad privacy choices. Flight Deck only requests ads that are suitable for a general audience.

This website

This section covers amateurgames1.com. You don't need an account to use it, and we don't ask for your name, email address or location.

Advertising and cookies

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, to measure them, and to personalise them based on your visits to this and other websites. To do this, Google may receive information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the page you're viewing and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and how Google uses cookies in advertising.

If you're in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you'll see a consent message from Google the first time you visit. Ads cookies for personalised ads are only used if you agree. If you don't, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use cookies only for things like frequency capping and fraud prevention. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy and cookie settings link that Google's message adds to the page.

Wherever you are, you can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, and learn about other ad networks' opt-outs at aboutads.info.

Fonts and hosting

Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare provides its domain name service. Like any web host, they may process your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and keep the site secure.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, so we can see which pages and games people use. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't store anything on your device, and doesn't track you across other websites. When a page loads, a small script sends Cloudflare basic details such as the page address, the referring site, your browser and device type, your rough location (country) and page load times. Cloudflare uses your IP address only to process the request and doesn't use it to identify you. We see only totals, never information about individual visitors. You can read more in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Scores and streaks

The free games on this website (Daily Departure, Quick Play and the airport page quizzes) save your scores and streaks in your own browser only, using local storage. That information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Emails you send us

If you email hello@amateurgames1.com, your message is forwarded to us by Cloudflare's email routing, and you'll get an automatic acknowledgement. We use your email address and message only to read your feedback and, where needed, to help with a problem. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

The apps are made for a general audience and contain no chat, sharing or social features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
