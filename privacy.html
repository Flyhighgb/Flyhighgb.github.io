Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy covers the iPhone apps Airport Codes Quiz and Airline Tails Quiz (the "apps"), published by Amateurgames1. It explains what information is used when you play and why.

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your scores and settings are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

Advertising

The apps are free and show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address, device type, operating system, ad interactions and, only if you allow tracking when asked, your device's advertising identifier. Google's use of this information is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can turn off personalised ads at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

This website

amateurgames1.com shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Where the law requires it, you'll be asked for consent before these cookies are used. You can opt out of personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, and read more in how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Children

The apps are rated 4+ and contain no chat, sharing or social features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
