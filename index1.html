About Airline Tails Quiz

The tail fin is the most recognisable part of an airliner. Plane spotters can name an airline from across an airport, just from the colours and logo on the tail. Airline Tails Quiz lets you test how good you are at it, with airport codes thrown in for extra challenge.

Three ways to play

Airline Tails shows you a tail design and four airlines to choose from. Europe Airport Codes and USA Airport Codes show a three-letter code and ask you to pick the right airport. Questions are shuffled every time, so no two rounds are the same, and big 2×2 answer buttons make it easy to play one-handed.

Made for the flight

Airline Tails Quiz works offline, so you can play in airplane mode, at the gate or on a long flight. Scores are kept on your device and there's no account to create.

Tips for spotting tails

  • Start with colour. Many airlines use the same main colour across the whole fleet.
  • Then look at the shape: a bird, a flag, a letter or an abstract pattern.
  • Group airlines by region. National carriers often use their country's flag colours.
  • For the airport-code rounds, learn the patterns in our guides, such as why US codes avoid N, K and W.
