PPT is Papeete (Tahiti)

An airport in French Polynesia. PPT is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it PPT?

The letters P, P and T all appear, in that order, in “Papeete (Tahiti)”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
