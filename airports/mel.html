MEL is Melbourne

An airport in Australia. MEL is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Airport

Why is it MEL?

The letters M, E and L all appear, in that order, in “Melbourne”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
