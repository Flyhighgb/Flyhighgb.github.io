TSA is Taipei Songshan

An airport in Taiwan. TSA is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it TSA?

The letters T, S and A all appear, in that order, in “Taipei Songshan”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
