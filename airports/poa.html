POA is Porto Alegre

An airport in Brazil. POA is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it POA?

The letters P, O and A all appear, in that order, in “Porto Alegre”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
