NCE is Nice Côte d'Azur

An airport in France. NCE is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

IATA code

Airport

Country
France
Region

Why is it NCE?

The letters N, C and E all appear, in that order, in “Nice Côte d'Azur”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
