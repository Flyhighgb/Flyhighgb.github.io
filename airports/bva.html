BVA is Paris Beauvais

An airport in France. BVA is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Airport

Why is it BVA?

The letters B, V and A all appear, in that order, in “Paris Beauvais”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
