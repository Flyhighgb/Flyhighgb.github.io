PVR is Puerto Vallarta

An airport in Mexico. PVR is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it PVR?

The letters P, V and R all appear, in that order, in “Puerto Vallarta”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
