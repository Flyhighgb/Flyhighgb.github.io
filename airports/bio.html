BIO is Bilbao

An airport in Spain. BIO is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Airport

Why is it BIO?

The letters B, I and O all appear, in that order, in “Bilbao”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
