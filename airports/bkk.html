BKK is Bangkok Suvarnabhumi

An airport in Thailand. BKK is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it BKK?

The letters B, K and K all appear, in that order, in “Bangkok Suvarnabhumi”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
