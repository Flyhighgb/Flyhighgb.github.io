NUE is Nuremberg

An airport in Germany. NUE is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it NUE?

The letters N, U and E all appear, in that order, in “Nuremberg”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
