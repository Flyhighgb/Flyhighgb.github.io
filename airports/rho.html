RHO is Rhodes

An airport in Greece. RHO is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Airport

Why is it RHO?

The letters R, H and O all appear, in that order, in “Rhodes”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
