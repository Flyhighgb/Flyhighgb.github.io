BTS is Bratislava

An airport in Slovakia. BTS is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it BTS?

The letters B, T and S all appear, in that order, in “Bratislava”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
