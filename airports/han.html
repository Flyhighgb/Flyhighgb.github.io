HAN is Hanoi

An airport in Vietnam. HAN is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it HAN?

The letters H, A and N all appear, in that order, in “Hanoi”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
