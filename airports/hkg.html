HKG is Hong Kong

An airport in Hong Kong. HKG is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it HKG?

The letters H, K and G all appear, in that order, in “Hong Kong”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
