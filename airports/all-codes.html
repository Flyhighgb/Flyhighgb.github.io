    About this list

    IATA, the International Air Transport Association, gives three-letter codes to airports with airline flights, and to many smaller fields, seaplane bases and heliports too. This list covers every place with a current IATA code. Airports tagged Full page link to a page of their own with the story behind the code, the codes people mix them up with and a quick quiz.

    A few codes cover a whole city rather than one airport, such as LON for London or NYC for New York. Booking sites use them to search several airports at once, so they aren't in this list of airports.

    Airport data comes from OurAirports, a community project that puts its data in the public domain. Codes do change, and a list this long can have mistakes. Last updated 17 November 2026. Spotted one? Email hello@amateurgames1.com.
