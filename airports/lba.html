LBA is Leeds Bradford

An airport in the United Kingdom. LBA is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

Why is it LBA?

The letters L, B and A all appear, in that order, in “Leeds Bradford”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
