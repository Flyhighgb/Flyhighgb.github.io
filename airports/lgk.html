LGK

LGK is Langkawi

An airport in Malaysia. LGK is its three-letter IATA code, the one on your boarding pass and bag tag.

IATA code

Airport

Country

Region

Why is it LGK?

The letters L, G and K all appear, in that order, in “Langkawi”, which makes this one of the easier codes to remember.
