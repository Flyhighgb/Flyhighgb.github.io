About Amateurgames1

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Amateurgames1 makes free games for people who love flying. If you've ever stared at a departures board trying to work out where SGN or CGK goes, or wondered why Toronto is YYZ, this site is for you.

Who's behind it

“I am an amateur developer that loves creating apps for people to enjoy.” Amateurgames1 is a one-person project, which is where the name comes from. Every game, guide and app on this site is made by the same developer, for fellow aviation fans.

What you'll find here

  • Daily Departure: five airport codes a day, the same puzzle for everyone, with a streak to keep.
  • Quick Play: unlimited ten-question rounds, by region or worldwide.
  • Guides: the stories behind airport codes, written to help you remember them.
  • Airports A–Z: a look-up of 374 airport codes, with the story behind the code where there is one.
  • Three free apps: Flight Deck: Aviation Quiz for iPhone, iPad and Android, and Airport Codes Quiz and Airline Tails Quiz for iPhone and iPad.

Why we make these games

Airport codes look random until you learn the stories behind them. ORD remembers an orchard, YYZ remembers a radio station, and LAX has an X just to fill a gap. Once you know the story, the code sticks. Every game and guide here is built around that idea: short, fair questions, and a reason behind every answer.

How we check facts

Every guide and airport story is researched before it goes live. Airport codes and names are checked against IATA's official airline and airport code search. Stories behind codes, dates and names are checked against at least one published reference, such as the airport's own history or an encyclopaedia entry. Each guide lists its sources at the end.

We only publish a code's story when we're confident it's right. Where the history is disputed, like the origin of the Y in Canadian codes, we say so. That's also why some airport pages give the facts without a story.

Each guide shows when it was published and when it was last updated. When a page changes in a meaningful way, its date changes too.

Corrections

Spotted a mistake, or know a story we've missed? Email hello@amateurgames1.com with the page and what you think is wrong. Every report is read. When we confirm an error, we fix it, aiming to do so within a few days, and update the page's date.

How the site is paid for

Everything here is free to play, with no account to create. The website and apps are supported by ads from Google. The browser games save your scores and streaks only on your own device. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Contact

For questions, feedback or help with an app, email hello@amateurgames1.com or see the support page.
