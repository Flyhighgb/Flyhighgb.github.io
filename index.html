Airport Codes Quiz

Learn IATA airport codes the fun way

Is LHR Heathrow or Gatwick? What about JFK, CDG or HND? Airport Codes Quiz turns the codes on your boarding pass into a quick, satisfying game. It's great for frequent flyers, aviation fans and student pilots.

  • 1,000 randomised multiple-choice questions
  • Airports in Europe, North America, South America and the Far East
  • 50-question rounds with high scores and a review of the codes you missed
  • Learn Codes browser to study before you play
  • Cartoon graphics and confetti when you finish a round
